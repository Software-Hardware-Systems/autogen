# Memory Dump: Sprint 0 Planning

## Product Vision & Value Proposition
- **Vision**: Create a semi-autonomous AI agent ecosystem that mirrors traditional business organizational structures, enabling a solo entrepreneur to manage complex projects and serve clients effectively.
- **Value Proposition**:
  - Extend capabilities beyond individual limitations.
  - Provide clients with sophisticated, coordinated AI solutions.
  - Demonstrate a new paradigm of human-AI collaboration.
- **Target Users**:
  - Solo entrepreneurs managing complex projects.
  - Small businesses needing expanded capabilities.
  - Early adopter clients in industries like 3D Printing, Roofing, Automotive, and Industrial Equipment Inspection.
